## What does it cost?

Vigia is free. You only pay for Jev usage with your own key: $0.042 per million input tokens, and output is free. A busy night of chat usually costs cents. See [Your keys](../keys/).

## What does it ask for on Twitch?

Reading and sending chat, deleting messages, timeouts and bans, and seeing your mods. Permanent bans are always a person's call.

## What if Jev gets it wrong?

Vigia only acts when the probability clears your threshold, and anything in between goes to Uncertain for a mod to decide. You also start in observe mode. See [How it decides](../how-it-works/).

## Do I need my own Twitch app?

Only to moderate your channel. It's free and takes a few minutes to create. To try Vigia with "Just watch a channel", a Jev key is enough.

## What does Jev see of my chat?

The text of each message and its context, never usernames. See [Privacy](../privacy/).

## Does it work in Spanish?

Yes. The app, the bot and the docs are in English and Spanish. Jev is less confident in Spanish, so expect more messages in Uncertain.

## Can I use it without the desktop app?

Yes, with [Docker](../docker/), on a [server](../server/), or from the [command line](../cli/).
