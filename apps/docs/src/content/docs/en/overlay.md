---
title: OBS overlay
description: Show highlighted messages on your stream with OBS.
---

1. Copy the overlay address from the dashboard (**Settings**) or from the tray menu.
2. In OBS, add a **Browser source** with that address. The background is transparent.

While you place the source, you can add these parameters to the address:

- `&preview=1` shows a sample card that stays on screen.
- `&pos=bl|br|bc|tl|tr` picks the corner: bottom left, bottom right, bottom center, top left, top right.

In **Settings** you also choose the card style, how many seconds it stays on screen, and whether highlights go out on their own or a mod approves them.
